bb2dash · mockup sheet 00

MVP plan — context, added features, mockup list

Synthesized from README, NOTES, PHASE2_FINDINGS, DATA_SYNTAX, migrations 001–004 and the Fall 2026 seeds (state as of 2026-09-02). All mockups use real seed rows; anything marked sample is illustrative.

What exists (data)
  • 7 course shells (GEO lecture + recitation linked via parent_course_id), 12 staff, 11 meeting patterns, 127 dated sessions, 75 readings.
  • 64 assignments; 28 carry Blackboard column ids + exact due_at. Facts vs. your state split: assignment_progress (status, priority, planned dates, score).
  • Grading is declarative: grading_schemes + grade_components with aggregation rules (rank_weighted, average_drop_lowest, normalized, sum) — 4 different models across 6 courses; IST 352 / 466 still unknown.
  • Blackboard capture: bb_content (139 nodes), announcements (8, with is_read), bb_files (45, catalogued but bytes not fetchable), sync_runs provenance.
  • Every fact row has source + confidence (confirmed / tentative / inferred); conflicts land in sync_runs.summary and must be surfaced.
Design constraints derived from it
  • Materials hub must map two locations: the Blackboard catalog (bb_files.source_url) and the local OneDrive course context/ copy. Needs a local_path per file (schema add).
  • Chronology is the spine: sessions, readings and assignments all key on dates → course page = one timeline with two lanes (lecture / assignment).
  • Confidence must be visible everywhere a date or weight is shown (tentative dates are common: IST 466 typos, IST 471 undated).
  • Planner status is a first-class control (progress_status selector) on every assignment surface, never buried.
  • Grade math differs per course, so the breakdown popout explains the rule, not just the number.
  • Single user, dark, dense (Nocturne 0.7× density). Desktop-first; local device.
